# Photo prompts for ChatGPT or Gemini

Paste each prompt into ChatGPT (image generation) or Gemini, one at a time. Download the result and send it back in the Claude chat. Claude saves it in this folder with the file name shown, compresses it, and the page picks it up automatically.

Tips:
- If a result isn't right, say what to change in plain words ("make the sky darker", "remove the car") instead of starting over.
- Don't ask for logos, readable signs or real people's faces.

---

## 1. Hero: `hero.webp` (landscape, 16:9)

> Photorealistic architectural photograph of a freshly renovated two-story single-family home at blue hour, shot from across the street at a slight low angle with a 35mm lens. The sky is a deep, clear navy blue fading lighter near the horizon. Warm amber light glows from every window, and soft exterior wall lights wash the facade. The house is modern craftsman: dark charcoal siding, clean white trim, a gable roof with one chimney, a wide front door and a short concrete path. The house sits in the right half of the frame. The left half is calm, dark sky and lawn, with empty space for text. Quiet, premium, editorial real estate photography, crisp detail, natural colors, no people, no cars, no text, no logos. Wide 16:9 landscape.
